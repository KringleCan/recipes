# Garlic Butter

Original recipe: https://www.allrecipes.com/recipe/36925/garlic-butter/

Servings: 16
Prep time: 10 min.
Cook time: 0 min.
Total time: 10 min.

## Ingredients:
- 1 cup unsalted butter, softened
- 1 tablespoon minced garlic, or more to taste
- ¼ cup grated Parmesan cheese
- 1 teaspoon garlic salt, or more to taste
- 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning, or more to taste
- ½ teaspoon ground black pepper, or more to taste
- ¼ teaspoon ground paprika, or more to taste

## Instructions:
1. Gather all ingredients.
2. Combine softened butter, Parmesan cheese, and minced garlic in a bowl until well combined.
3. Mix in garlic salt, Italian seasoning, pepper, and paprika until smooth and well combined.
4. Taste and adjust seasonings.

## Notes:
- You can use any butter you like. Also, fresh or minced garlic in a jar works well. Adjust the amount of garlic to your taste.
- Freeze this garlic butter in portions for easy use when you need it.
