# Creamy Chipotle Lime Chicken Pasta Salad

Original recipe: [https://www.allrecipes.com/recipe/8691/chicken-enchiladas-i/](https://www.instagram.com/p/DaQnmWBNH5s/)

Servings: 
Prep time:  min.
Cook time:  min.
Total time:  min.

## Indredients:
### Chicken:
- 1 lb boneless, skinless chicken thighs
- 1/2 tsp avocado oil
- Juice of 1 lime
- 1 tbsp chipotle powder (or smoked paprika)
- 1 tbsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tbsp onion powder
- Salt
- pepper
- chili flakes

### Dressing:
- 1/2 cup plain Greek yogurt
- 3 tbsp avocado oil mayo
- A handful fresh mint
- small handful basil
- 1/2 jalapeño
- Juice of 1 large lime
- 1 garlic clove
- Salt
- Pepper
- 2 tbsp water to thin out, as needed

### Salad:
- 8 oz Kaizen protein pasta or any pasta you like
- 2 cups romaine, finely chopped
- 3/4 cup charred corn
- 4 scallions, thinly sliced
- 1/2 red onion, finely diced
- Extra cilantro for garnish
- Optional: jalapeño, cucumber, or feta,

## Instructions:
### Chicken:
1. Season chicken with spices, oil, and lime juice. Mix well.
2. Roast at 400F for 30-35 mins in air fryer. Let cool slightly, then chop.

### Dressing:
1. Combine everything into a small food processor and process really well. Dont forget the salt! If desired, thin out the sauce with a splash of water.

### Salad:
1. Cook 8 oz of your fav high-protein or lowcarb pasta. I used my @kaizenfoodco pasta because it’s both high protein (20g) and low carb (6 net carbs).
2. Drain and rinse.
3. Prep your lettuce, onion, scallions, and jalapeno/cucumbers if using.
4. In a large bowl or jar, combine the cooked pasta, diced chicken, chopped romaine, scallions, charred corn, and a handful of finely chopped cilantro.
5. Pour the dressing and toss well.
6. Top with feta, cilantro, and a few extra jalapeño slices if you like a little extra heat.
