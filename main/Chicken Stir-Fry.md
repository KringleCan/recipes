# Chicken Stir-Fry

Original recipe: https://www.delish.com/cooking/recipe-ideas/a45362568/best-chicken-stir-fry-recipe/

Servings: 4
Prep time: 10 min.
Cook time: 30 min.
Total time: 40 min.

## Indredients:
- 1/2 cup reduced-sodium soy sauce
- 2 tbsp honey
- 2 tsp toasted sesame oil
- 1 tbsp canola oil
- 1 head broccoli, cut into small florets
- 1 bell pepper, seeds and ribs removed, chopped
- 1 cup of shredded carrots
- 2 cloves garlic, finely chopped
- 1 lb. boneless, skinless chicken breast, cut into 1" pieces
- 1/3 cup cashews
- Ground black pepper

## Instructions:
1. In a large skillet over medium heat, heat oil.
2. In a small bowl, whisk soy sauce, honey, and sesame oil and set aside.
3. Add broccoli, bell pepper, carrots, and garlic to the skillet. Cook, stirring frequently, until softened, about 5 minutes.
4. Add chicken to the skillet. Cook, tossing occasionally, until golden brown and cooked through, about 8 minutes. Stir in cashews; season with pepper.
5. Pour sauce into skillet and bring to a simmer. Cook, stirring occasionally, until thickened, about 5 minutes.
